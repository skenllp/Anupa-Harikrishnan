Our Story

Two Families, One Journey

Every beautiful beginning starts with a moment of faith, a family's blessing, and the promise of a shared future. As two families come together in love and tradition, Harikrishnan M S and Anupa Vinod begin a new chapter filled with hope, happiness, and lifelong companionship. Surrounded by the warmth of their loved ones, they look forward to a journey built on mutual respect, cherished memories, and countless joyful moments

How It Began

Every meaningful journey has a humble beginning. What started as a meeting between two families soon became a bond strengthened by shared values, warm conversations, and the blessings of elders. With each passing day, the foundation for a beautiful future grew stronger.

The Blessing

With the love and guidance of their families, and the blessings of Lord Guruvayurappan, this union was embraced with joy and gratitude. Rooted in tradition and celebrated with affection, the ring exchange marks the coming together of two families in a bond that will last a lifetime

Forever, From Here

On Sunday, 30 August 2026, during the auspicious ring exchange ceremony, Harikrishnan and Anupa begin their journey together. As they step into this new chapter, they seek the blessings, love, and good wishes of everyone who joins them in celebrating this special occasion
